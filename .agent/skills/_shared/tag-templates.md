# Ready-made 13-tag templates (jewelry)

Built 2026-10-21 from a 16-term / 640-listing sample of Etsy's top-40 search results (`sort_on=score`), then analysed with
`scripts/keyword-stats.mjs`. Slots and rules: "Tag slot model" in `etsy-seo-standards.md`. **These are starting points, not
answers:** swap the motif words per product, then re-check against a fresh pull for that exact product (`rank-first-title-guide.md`).
Each template is dated; templates older than **30 days** are re-validated before reuse (search results drift weekly).

Every tag is <=20 characters, lowercase, unique within the set. The percentage is the share of the researched term's top 40
that used that exact tag (sample date 2026-10-20/21). `own angle` tags have no evidence and count against the max of 3.
